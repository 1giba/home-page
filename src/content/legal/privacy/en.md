## 1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Code Happy Tecnologia Ltda., registered under CNPJ 37.833.013/0001-51, handles the personal data of visitors who access this website or get in touch to learn about its technology advisory, AI strategy and engineering, and fractional CTO services.

Its purpose is to explain, transparently, which information is processed, for what purposes, and how you can exercise your rights.

## 2. Personal data we process

Depending on how you interact with this website, we may process the following information:

- **Contact details:** what you send through the contact form: name, email, company (optional), the service you are interested in and your message. If you write directly to contato@codehappy.com.br, we receive your email address and the content of your message.
- **Professional information:** details about your company, projects and business needs that you share while in contact with us.
- **Scheduling information:** data you provide to book meetings through Calendly.
- **Technical browsing data:** when you open the website, your browser sends the hosting provider technical data such as IP address, date and time, the page requested and browser information, needed to deliver the website.

We ask only for information relevant to the purposes described in this policy.

## 3. Purposes of processing

Personal data may be used to:

- Reply to requests sent through the contact form or by email.
- Schedule and hold introductory and diagnostic meetings.
- Assess opportunities and, where relevant, prepare a service proposal.
- Comply with legal obligations.
- Deliver the website and keep it running.

We do not use the data for purposes incompatible with those disclosed to you.

## 4. Contact form and email

Messages sent through the contact form pass through FormSubmit, which delivers them to the company's email address, contato@codehappy.com.br, hosted on Google Workspace.

This information may be used to reply to your request, keep a record of the conversation, and follow up on a possible business negotiation.

Sending a message does not automatically mean hiring any service.

## 5. Scheduling meetings through Calendly

The links to book a meeting lead to Calendly. When you use it, you may provide the information the platform requests, such as your name, email address, availability and details about the meeting.

The processing of this data is also subject to Calendly's privacy practices. See [Calendly's privacy notice](https://calendly.com/legal/privacy-notice).

## 6. Data sharing

Personal data is processed by the technology providers needed to run the website and to handle communication and scheduling:

- **FormSubmit:** receives contact form messages and delivers them by email.
- **Google Workspace:** our email provider, for receiving and managing contact messages.
- **Calendly:** provides and manages bookings.
- **Vercel:** hosts and serves the website.

We do not sell visitors' personal data.

Data may also be shared when needed to comply with legal obligations or with orders from competent authorities.

## 7. International data transfer

These providers are foreign companies and may process or store data outside Brazil.

## 8. Cookies and browser storage

This website uses no cookies and no visitor statistics, advertising or tracking tools.

If you use the light/dark theme button, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage (localStorage). It is never sent to us and can be cleared in your browser settings.

## 9. Retention and deletion

We keep contact details and messages for as long as they are needed to reply to you and to follow up on a possible proposal. When they are no longer needed, they are deleted, except where the law provides for them to be kept.

## 10. Your rights

Under art. 18 of Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD), you may request:

- Confirmation that your personal data is processed.
- Access to the data processed.
- Correction of incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data.
- Anonymization, blocking or deletion of data in the cases provided by law.
- Data portability.
- Information about the sharing of your data.
- Withdrawal of consent, where processing is based on it.
- Other rights provided by the LGPD.

Requests will be assessed and answered in accordance with the applicable legal requirements.

## 11. Privacy contact

For questions or requests about the processing of personal data, contact us:

- **Company:** Code Happy Tecnologia Ltda.
- **CNPJ:** 37.833.013/0001-51
- **Email:** contato@codehappy.com.br

You can also use this channel for requests about your rights under the LGPD.

## 12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in how the website works, in the company's activities or in legal requirements. The current version will be available on this page, with the date of its last update.

## 13. Governing law

This policy is interpreted in accordance with applicable Brazilian law, in particular Law No. 13,709/2018 (General Data Protection Law, LGPD).
