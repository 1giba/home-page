# Source: SPEC §3.7.1 (target customer, problems, scope, business value). Provisional copy (E3).
title: Fractional CTO & Technology Leadership
summary: Part-time senior technology leadership for companies that depend on software and have no CTO.
metaTitle: Fractional CTO
metaDescription: 'Part-time senior technology leadership for companies without a CTO: roadmap, architecture decisions, vendor oversight and engineering hiring.'
order: 1
audience:
  - Founders and business owners whose product or operations depend on software.
  - Companies without a senior technical leader, or between technical leaders.
problems:
  - No one owns technical strategy.
  - Unclear build, buy or outsource decisions.
  - Dependence on an agency or vendor.
  - Unpredictable delivery.
  - Hiring engineers without the ability to evaluate them.
  - Technical questions from investors or stakeholders without a good answer.
scope:
  - Ongoing, part-time technology leadership.
  - Technical roadmap and architecture decisions.
  - Vendor and agency oversight.
  - 'Hiring support: role definition and interviews.'
  - Delivery process.
  - Representing technology to stakeholders and investors.
  - Where it makes sense, a planned transition to a full-time CTO.
value:
  - Decisions made by someone experienced and accountable for them.
  - Lower risk of costly rewrites or vendor lock-in.
  - A clearer view of what your technology spend is producing.
implementation: 'When the work includes implementation, I also build alongside your team.'
