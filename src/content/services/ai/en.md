title: AI Strategy & Engineering

metaTitle: AI Strategy & Engineering
metaDescription: Find where AI pays off in your product or operations, then take those use cases to production with cost, quality and risk under control.
order: 2
audience:
  - Companies that want to apply AI, typically large language models (LLMs), to their product or operations.
  - Companies that need to decide whether and where to use AI before building.
problems:
  - It is unclear which use cases are worth it.
  - 'The prototype works but production does not: cost, latency, accuracy or data privacy.'
  - There is no way to evaluate the quality of what the AI produces.
  - Integrating AI into an existing product without destabilizing it.
scope:
  - Use-case discovery and prioritization.
  - Feasibility and cost assessment.
  - 'Architecture for AI features: retrieval, evaluation, guardrails and observability.'
  - Hands-on implementation, or leading the team that implements.
value:
  - Investment directed to AI uses with a credible case.
  - Features that hold up in production.
  - Known running costs.
implementation: I can build it myself, or your team builds it with me leading it technically.
